## Full Description

Pick your cat. Chase the mouse. Score points!

Mouse Catch is a simple, addictive arcade game where you control a cat chasing a quick little mouse across an open field. Turn with left and right, move forward, and score each time you touch the mouse.

FEATURES:
- 3 unique cats to choose from — Marmalade, Shadow, and Midnight
- Progressive difficulty — the mouse gets faster and smarter each level
- Points system with time bonuses and high score tracking
- Beautiful hand-drawn characters and smooth animations
- Works offline — play anywhere, anytime
- No ads. No in-app purchases. Just fun.

HOW TO PLAY:
- Pick a cat and tap Start
- Use the on-screen d-pad to turn and move
- Walk into the mouse to catch it and score points
- Catch all the mice before time runs out to advance

Fun for kids and adults alike. Simple to learn, hard to master.
